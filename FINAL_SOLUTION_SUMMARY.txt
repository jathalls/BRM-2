  ❌ Used: MACCATALYST, IOS, ANDROID
  ✅ Correct: __MACCATALYST__, __IOS__, __ANDROID__

.NET MAUI requires the double underscore (__) prefix!

   - Changed: IOS → __IOS__

   - Changed: IOS → __IOS__
   - Changed: MACCATALYST → __MACCATALYST__

3. Views/AudioPlayer.xaml.cs
   - Changed: IOS → __IOS__
   - Changed: MACCATALYST → __MACCATALYST__
   - Changed: ANDROID → __ANDROID__
